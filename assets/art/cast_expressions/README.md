## Shared prompt

Use case: identity-preserve. Edit the supplied approved large VN portrait into ONE expression variant for Demonologist. The reference is authoritative for the character's exact identity, age, face structure, skin, hair arrangement, outfit construction, embroidery, palette and hand-inked textured papercraft illustration style. Preserve them. Keep the original 1024x1536 transparent portrait canvas, waist-up crop, torso anchor, head size, hairstyle clearance and overall placement; expression and subtle head/shoulder movement may change naturally but avoid a large camera turn or reframing. Keep hands tucked below frame, no extra props, text, borders or background. Genuine alpha transparency. Use emotionally readable eyes, brows and mouth rather than simply reproducing the reference expression. No photorealism, 3D, heavy new crosshatching or redesign. Facial feeling expresses the person's outward delivery, never hidden guilt or truth.
