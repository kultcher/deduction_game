### Agnes

Use case: identity-preserve.
Asset type: high-resolution visual novel character portrait for the game Demonologist, standalone transparent PNG.
Primary request: Enlarge and faithfully develop the reference character into a large waist-up portrait, retaining the exact existing illustrated papercraft aesthetic. This is the same character, same outfit and same expression, adapted for close-up dialogue, not a redesign.
Style/medium: match the reference's hand-cut layered paper illustration, warm subtle paper grain, inked dark contour lines, angular overlapping color shapes, restrained flat cel shading and printed storybook detail. Keep the stylization and original facial proportions; no photorealism, no 3D, no glossy digital painting.
Composition: portrait-oriented high-resolution canvas, ideally 1024 by 1536 or larger. Waist-up, entire hair and shoulders comfortably inside the canvas, head in upper third, character fills most of the frame, modest transparent padding around silhouette. Continue existing clothing naturally into lower torso. Preserve original three-quarter head angle and gaze. No props, hands can remain out of view. Clean transparent cutout with no scene, no text, no nameplate, no circular token base or frame, no cast shadow outside silhouette. Preserve real alpha transparency. Ignore any tiny fragments of neighboring characters at edges of the reference; depict exactly one person.
Invariants: same age, skin tone, face, hair arrangement, jewelry, garment palette, garment construction, mood and expression. Crisp intentional details at the larger scale without making the art busy or changing its style.
Subject: Agnes. Older warm-faced woman with medium warm brown skin, softly lined smiling face, dark braided hair coiled into a low bun and a broad cream-gray streak sweeping across the front, gold teardrop earrings.
