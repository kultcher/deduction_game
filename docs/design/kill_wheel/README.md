# Night kill wheel and execution mark: clips (Limner, Nov 22)

These are the clips for Michael's approval of the look, from PR #530 (`claude/kill-wheel`). This
is a mock branch: it never merges.

## Files

- **`kill_wheel_normal.mp4`:** a kill (Greta), then the no-kill fakeout (Kiran), then an
  execution (Dorian), at full motion. About 13 s.
- **`kill_wheel_reduced.mp4`:** the same three in reduced motion. About 7 s.
- **`frames_kill.png`:** stills of the kill: the spin, landing, the slice isolated, the stab
  with its flash, and the red X.
- **`frames_fakeout_and_execution.png`:** stills of the fakeout's bump, bounce and fall,
  then the execution's grey and the blue X.

The stills were taken before two fixes in #530. They show the faded slices' gold rings
still visible, and the red flash covering only the wheel's square. In the clips the rings
fade away and the flash covers the whole screen.

Recorded with `tools/kill_wheel_capture.gd` at 1920×1080, scaled to 1280 wide.
