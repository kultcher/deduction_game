# Morning grade options for the map lighting (Limner, Nov 27)

These sheets are for Michael's choice. This is a mock branch: it never merges. Each sheet
shows the current morning, the option, and the afternoon (the painted map as it is), with a
close-up of the central green, paths and lots below. Case 7 at 1920×1080.

- **B, softer gold:** the same golden-hour idea, with half the warm lift and less yellow in
  the highlights. The paths stay warm, but their paper texture returns.
- **C, rose dawn:** no warm lift, and a rosier, cooler early light. It reads as early
  morning more than as gold.
- **D, light touch:** barely off the painted map, with just a little warmth. The most
  texture, and the least sense of a time of day.

Limner's recommendation: **B**. It keeps what Argon was going for, a golden morning that
reads differently from the afternoon, while giving the paper back.
