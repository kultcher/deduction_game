10-NPC town, dynamic script, the first playtest of the Burdens prototype. Build `ac3ec83`,
which is on `mock/burdens-slot`, not main. I replayed it at that build in a worktree. The
replay is faithful: every vote matches, and so does the case-board number of your day-3
case. The only difference is the known meeting-testimony split at seq 150.

## The main issue: a Minion double-claimed its own teammate, then had nothing to say

**Two bugs (Beadle).** Together they gave evil one blank player whose claim got its
teammate hanged.

1. **The deliberate double claim doesn't check whose claim it's copying.**
   - **What it is:** `choose_double_claim` (`sim/mind_meeting.gd` ~line 420) is your Nov 19
     mechanic: a Minion hears a claim and knowingly claims the same gift, to cast doubt on
     the rival. It excludes the Demon, a Scion, a Minion that has already claimed, and one
     committed to a told fake reading.
   - **What's missing:** it never asks whether the rival is a teammate. Fenwick won his 1-in-20
     roll (seq 145) and copied Merrin's Archivist claim. Merrin was his fellow Minion.
   - **What it cost evil:** both were nominated for the clash. Lian cited "they claim a gift
     Merrin claims", and Kiran said the same of Merrin. Merrin was hanged.
   - **Fix:** one line, return false when `Evil.is_teammate(w, l, rival.id)`.
2. **A Minion that switches mid-game to a first-night gift has no fake reading to give.**
   - **What happened:** `_change_bluff` dropped Fenwick's Registrar fabrications. The
     Archivist's `generate_bluff_reading` (`sim/roles/archivist.gd:45`) only makes one on the
     first night (`if w.day != 0: return {}`). So Fenwick held an Archivist claim with no
     reading for the rest of the game.
   - **Which paths hit it:** the double claim, and `switch_taken_bluff` (a bluff someone else
     claimed first) whenever the spare is a first-night gift.
   - **Fix:** fabricate the first-night reading at the switch.

**Fixed (Nov 22) by Beadle on `mock/burdens-slot`:**
- `6e950bbc`: never double-claims a teammate, and a late first-night bluff gets its reading.
- `d04f7f7a`: no double claim once the Minion has privately told anyone another gift.

Beadle confirmed by replay that day 1's clash is gone. The podium guard and the
resistance-line fix below are separate and still open.

**What the blank claim did downstream.** Several of your other notes follow from bug 2:
- **No press on his role on day 1:** `MindPodium.options` offers "press role" or "press
  readings" to a public claimant only if they have readings left to give or an ongoing
  gift. Fenwick had neither, so the game refused: "has already told the town their gift and
  everything it showed them". He had told nothing.
- **Day 2, `press card 37`:** "I've told the town everything my gift has shown me"
  (`podium_nothing_new`).
- **Day 3, privately:** "my gift has shown me nothing yet!" An Archivist's gift has nothing
  "yet" to come: it shows its one reading on the first night.
- **Once bug 2 is fixed,** these become a real reading. Still worth a guard: the podium
  should offer the role press to a claimant who has never given a reading. Today it counts
  "no readings left to give" the same as "gave them all".
