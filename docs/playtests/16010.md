## Michael's questions

### 1. Did the case against Odette do anything?

**Short answer: no, not against Odette. The town hanged her on Constance's fake Registrar
reading and a lie from Isolde.** Your case was weighed and rejected. When the listeners
weighed it, it pointed them toward Lian instead.

**What the case was.** Card #95: "Odette is a minion". It challenged her Archivist reading
as a lie, backed by three cards:
- Edda's Gravedigger reading that Agnes was the Chronicler;
- Agnes's private Chronicler claim;
- Lian's Confessor claim.

You laid it before the town with `accuse 95` during the vote on Odette.

**How each listener judged it** (`explain_case --card 95`):

| Listener | Verdict | "Odette is a minion", before → after | Suspicion of Odette |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fenwick | reject | 0.23 → 0.35 (weak) | 0.47 → 0.47 |
| Hollis | reject | 0.26 → 0.32 (none) | 0.31 → 0.31 |
| Nisha | reject | 0.31 → 0.40 (weak) | 0.27 → 0.28 |
| Edda | reject | 0.20 → 0.34 (weak) | 0.18 → 0.18 |
| Dorian | reject (partial in play) | 0.25 → 0.38 (weak) | 0.28 → 0.29 |

- **Why it couldn't land.** Each listener looked for another explanation that fits, and
  found it in Odette being honest.
  - **Her reading was the best-supported card in the case.** They rated it reliable
    (0.68–0.90).
  - **The Agnes cards point to Lian, not away from Odette.** With Agnes confirmed as the
    Chronicler, an honest Archivist reading makes Lian the Poisoner.
  - **Lian's Confessor claim** is a bare claim (reliability 0.50) and can't outweigh a
    confirmed reading.

  So the explanation they preferred moved toward Lian, by +0.13 to +0.24 depending on the
  listener. Lian was in fact the Poisoner.
- **What the Test juror's "didn't support" meant.** It is the same verdict.
- **The case left no trace in suspicion.** Taking it out of what each mind had heard moves
  nobody's suspicion of Odette by even 0.005 (`explain_case --belief odette --at 545`).
- **Not tested:** what happens with any one supporting card removed. The tool can't build
  the case with a support missing.

**What actually hanged her.** Suspicion of Odette just before the vote, by what drove it
(`--belief odette --at 545`):

| Voter | Suspicion | Biggest causes |
|---|---|---|
| Fenwick | 0.47 | Constance's fake Registrar reading +0.18; Isolde's lie that Odette was out on Chapel Hill on the first night +0.14 |
| Hollis | 0.31 | Constance's reading +0.10 |
| Dorian | 0.29 | Constance's reading +0.09 |
| Nisha | 0.28 | Constance's two readings +0.02 each (most of hers is her starting suspicion) |

- **Your accusation and vote.** On top of that, your `accuse` added about 0.08–0.09 to each
  good voter's score and your yes vote about 0.06. Citing #95 added nothing: it's the same
  size as your card-less accusation on day 1.
- **Neither was decisive.** Without either, Fenwick, Hollis, Nisha and Dorian would all
  still have been over their thresholds. Hollis would have been closest, at 0.58 against
  0.52. Add the three evil votes and Odette had 7, where 6 were needed.
- **The evil play was careful.**
  - Constance chose a target whose real reading pointed at her teammate.
  - She brought out her fake Registrar reading at the nomination (seq 509–515).
  - The demon on the block (Isolde) voted to replace herself with Odette.
- **Under the guardrails this is working as intended.** The town was wrong for an
  understandable reason, a fabricated reading from a claimed Registrar. The case was
  solvable: in your Isolde case on day 3, and in Odette's own nomination of Lian, which
  got 2 votes.
