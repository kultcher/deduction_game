## Bugs, by owner

- **Beadle:**
  1. `choose_double_claim` (`sim/mind_meeting.gd`) lets a Minion double-claim its own
     teammate. Fenwick copied Merrin's Archivist claim, and both were nominated for it;
     Merrin was hanged. It needs an `Evil.is_teammate` check.
  2. After a mid-game bluff switch to a first-night gift (by double claim or
     `switch_taken_bluff`), the Minion has no fake reading:
     `archivist.generate_bluff_reading` only fabricates on night 0. Fenwick never gave a
     reading. That's also why you couldn't press his role, why he said "told everything",
     and why he later said "shown nothing yet".
  3. `MindPodium.options` treats "no readings left to give" as "gave them all". A claimant
     who never gave a reading should still be pressable.
  4. `vote_resistance_lines` lets the person on the block explain a no on the
     counter-nominee (Jory: "No way I'm hanging Kiran!"). It should skip `res.prev_block`.
  - **Update (Nov 22):** 1 and 2 are fixed on `mock/burdens-slot` (`6e950bbc`, `d04f7f7a`),
    confirmed by replay. 3, 4 and the agreeing-readings selection are fixed in `e2f89ad1`
    (same branch). I sent findings to Beadle, Herald and Vicar directly. Herald's wording
    items and Vicar's design question are still open.
