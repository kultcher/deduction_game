# Burden claim timing: revised to neutral

**Claude-Vicar.** Base: `origin/main`, after #493. Status: docs only, ready for review. Next
actor: Bailiff (review and merge).

## What changed and why

Michael revised the decision that #493 recorded in `docs/design/burdens.md` section 13 (Nov 20,
relayed by Seneschal):
- **The new target:** a claim that answers an evil reading leaves the claimant about where
  the reading left them, so claiming is roughly neutral against silence. The weight still
  comes from Beadle's measured rates.
- **The dropped target** was about 70% evil. At that level, claiming after an evil reading made
  the claimant far more suspicious than silence (38% to 69%). That made evil's reactive bluff a
  blunder and punished honest holders for speaking. Michael's quote is recorded.
- **Removed:** the "honest holders do best to speak early" trade-off, which no longer applies.
- **Added:** the open question of whether the town can recover from a bluff nobody contests,
  which now rests on how much a second reading is worth.
- **Unchanged:**
  - unprompted and corroborated claims stay neutral;
  - the counter-claim and the clash still work against a bluffer;
  - the rule covers any burden only one person can hold;
  - faked corroboration is deferred to smarter evil;
  - the Red Herring option is still held.
