## What changed

This is the player's half of `docs/design/withheld_readings.md` §5. Michael decided it on Nov 22: the name "Call on", once per meeting, outside a nomination.

- **The button:** "Call on…" is a meeting action after "Present a deduction…". It opens a picker of the living, "Call on whom?", and greys out once used, with its reason as the tooltip. Silenced NPCs are greyed out in the picker.
- **The command:** `call <name>` (`GameMeeting.call_on`) logs "You call on X to share anything they haven't yet told the town.", then plays whatever `MindCallOn.respond` returns: its lines, and any board facts it gives.
  - **Once per meeting:** recorded in `w.meeting["called_on"]`, which every meeting starts without.
  - **Silenced:** a silenced voice can't be called on, and trying doesn't spend the call.
  - **`call_on_rejection(g, n)`:** gives the reason for both the button and the command.
- **`sim/mind_call_on.gd`:** a stub of Beadle's function, agreed with him on Nov 22. It always answers `call_on_nothing` ("I've told the town all I know.") and moves no RNG. Its header documents the contract Beadle fills in: `{lines, facts, outcome, reason, lie}`.
