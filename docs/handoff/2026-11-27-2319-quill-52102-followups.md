## What changed and why

Michael's answers on Nov 27, through Seneschal:

1. **A block reading is about the block's residents** (52102 finding 5: card #76, a Surveyor's "all the folk living on the Market share the same alignment", presented against Lian, who lives there; Lian answered "That card isn't about me").
   - A new role hook, `residents_block(data)`, is empty by default. The Surveyor's parity reading returns its block; a Surveyor count names no block.
   - Three places use it:
     - **Presenting** (`GamePresentation._route_present`): a resident is now a subject, so presenting the card to them puts it to them directly.
     - **The about-me check** (`sim/mind_press.gd` `press`): a resident pressed with it answers it, instead of "What has that to do with me?". This is Beadle's file; the change is the one condition, and the NPC reads its own home.
     - **`CardModel.about`:** the board's links and the profile's "what's been said or seen of them". It reads a presentation-only `CardModel.homes` that `Game.new_case` sets, which the simulation never reads.
2. **Pass 3's argued-Delusion odds are removed** (`workshop/source_doubt.gd`). They never fired in play, because the player's word card carries a burden premise. With them goes `DOUBT_ARG_DELUSIONAL_SCALE`, their only user, and `tuning_catalogue.md` is regenerated. Lantern's `delusion_odds` hook in the judge stays: it's his, and `test_shared_delusion` tests it directly.
3. **"It rests on N cards together." is cut** from the case-strength reason (`workshop/case_eval.gd`; case #136). A case with several pivotal cards now gives no reason line. "It rests on one card." and "Several lines of evidence agree." stay.
4. **BACKLOG:** "A case-strength helper that names what would firm the case up" `[decided: direction]`, under Cases and the Workshop. I own what's computed; Herald owns how it's shown.
