## What changed

- **`MindCallOn.respond`** replaces Herald's stub (#542), on the agreed contract:
  - **Shares** what it hasn't told the town when the normal disclosure rules say yes, with a
    light push:
    - its readings: already out with its gift, it shares them as at its turn; otherwise the
      usual "say it before the town?" decision decides;
    - a late reader's readings it heard;
    - night sightings it would volunteer.
    - Said as `call_on_share`, then the items in their usual lines.
  - **Nothing to tell:** `call_on_nothing`.
  - **Holding back, good:** a visible refusal.
    - `withheld_defer_burden` for a Rumormonger's reading (with Burdens, also the Unrequited
      shielding its beloved);
    - `withheld_defer_friend` for a reading naming a friend;
    - `withheld_defer_distrust` ("Demonologist") when it distrusts the player
      (`Tuning.Trust.DISTRUST_EXPLAIN_THRESHOLD`, reused);
    - `withheld_deflect` otherwise.
  - **Holding back, evil:** says it has told all it knows. That's a logged lie (`lie: true`,
    the bluff kept back), per the contract agreed with Herald.
- **The push:** `Tuning.Meeting.CALL_ON_PRESSURE_BUMP` (new, 0.3, in pressure units).
  - `_public_claim_decision` gains a `called_on` part, as that much more pressure would add.
  - `_volunteer_night_sightings` lowers its bar by as much.
  - Both take the bump as an optional argument, default 0, so the ordinary meeting is
    unchanged.

## Small calls made on Michael's behalf

- **The bump's size, 0.3** (Michael left it to the builder). It adds about 0.12–0.24 to the
  claim decision, depending on composure, like a moderate accusation's pressure. That's light:
  an NPC set on hiding still mostly hides.
- **Evil's refusal is the quiet "nothing" lie, not a deflection.** A bluffer that won't commit
  yet keeps its cover the way a liar would, and the log says why.
