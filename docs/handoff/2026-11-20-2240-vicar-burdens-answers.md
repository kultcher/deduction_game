## What changed and why

Michael answered the 20 open questions in `docs/design/burdens.md` (Nov 20). The note now
records them:

- **The script:**
  - at most one hidden burden, none required, and any hidden one is named on the script;
  - one burden per script at both town sizes for now;
  - a Baron-like Minion could add only known burdens, and is out of scope.
- **The slate:**
  - **Delusional** stays as built.
  - **Marked** has tiered disclosure: about 15% announce it publicly, 40% share it privately
    (with a trusted NPC or the player), and 45% hold it until nominated or named in an evil
    reading.
  - **Unrequited:** the beloved is random and doesn't know. Trust damps their suspicion, with
    custom damping only if needed. They defend within reason and may hide evidence.
  - **Ill-Omened:** try the "everyone is drunk" version first. All good gifts are drunk and
    evil is unaffected; only a hanging sets it off; the Demon doesn't know; it's always cited
    before an execution.
- **Reveals, bluffing and belief:**
  - a confidant chosen by the usual friend rules, with a chance to start knowing and more
    chances through trusted talks;
  - a small town-wide trust cost for learning publicly, with confidants exempt;
  - any known burden can be bluffed, chosen from public knowledge;
  - evil doesn't know the holders;
  - a general allowance of about 10%, scaled by claims and Rumormonger reads;
  - the per-case either/or only if needed.
- **Retiring:** the Lovers, Sleepwalker 1.0 (its code kept where useful for 2.0), the flavour
  secrets and the no-gift denial.
- **The gifts that read secrets stay as they are,** since a holder is always good and a bluff
  is only a bluff.
- **Scope:** plain folk are separate; the code keeps "secret"; the freed solver room most
  likely goes to larger towns.

Section 4's workstreams are updated to match. Section 8 lists the five questions these
answers raise:
1. Is a known burden named on the script?
2. Do Ill-Omened's drunk readers know their gift failed?
3. Is Marked's private share with the player its own tier?
4. Does Unrequited's hiding go through "Good NPCs lie tactically"?
5. Does the allowance drop once one burden is claimed?
