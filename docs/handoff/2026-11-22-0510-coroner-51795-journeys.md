# 2026-11-22, Claude-Coroner: NPC journeys for playtest 51795 (`claude/playtest-51795`, PR #526)

- **Base:** main. **Status:** docs only, added to the open review PR #526. **Next actor:**
  Bailiff (merge), then Lantern and Beadle.
- **What:** `docs/playtests/51795_journeys.md`, at Michael's request. It follows Kiran,
  Isolde, Odette and Dorian: their suspicions and what drove them, how they saw the player,
  and their votes and case stances. I replayed at `ac3ec83` and took leave-one-out
  attributions at each meeting.

## Findings, by owner

- **Lantern:**
  1. Being accused or nominated by someone you know is wrong (you know you're good) lowers
     trust in them but never raises suspicion of them. Kiran's trust in Odette was 0.0 and
     his suspicion of her 0.00, after three days of her accusations.
  2. A suspected reader's readings still clear others. Jory's (the Demon's) day-1 Dowser
     count cleared Fenwick and Odette for several minds, including Kiran, who suspected
     Jory at 0.52.
  3. Dorian's suspicion of Fenwick stayed at 0.23–0.29 all game, the two claims pushing up
     and Jory's count pushing down. A rival claimant proven evil didn't loosen the clash
     for him, though it did for Kiran and Isolde.
- **Beadle:**
  1. Friendship and trust aren't in the vote score. Isolde (trust in Kiran 0.95, who had
     just spoken for him) came within 0.03 of voting to hang him, on baseline suspicion
     plus town pressure.
  2. Question: grief over a hanged friend never fades. Odette's trust in the player stayed
     at 0.32 from day 1 to the end.
- **Working as intended:** Odette's loyalty to Merrin turned Kiran's Gravedigger reading
  against him, and Isolde's Medium reading broke it on day 3.

## Measured, and on what

- **Seed:** this one log, 51795.
- **Method:** for 4 good NPCs at 9 meeting points, I took each item a mind had heard out
  one at a time and measured how far its suspicions moved. Votes come from the log's vote
  breakdowns.
- **Limit:** taking items out one at a time understates a suspicion built from several
  pieces that each make the others redundant.
