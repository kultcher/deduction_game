## What changed and why

This is part of the cleanup rollout (Michael, Nov 19). Of game.gd's 162 functions, 131 are one-line forwards left from the earlier split (`GameBoard.add_card(self, …)` and so on). Michael wants callers pointed straight at the module that does the work, and the forwards removed. Seneschal approved the plan as scoped, as a small call on Michael's behalf.

This first PR deletes the 25 forwards that nothing calls: not game.gd, the game modules, sim/, ui/ or the tests. Nothing passes them as a callable or calls them by name. Their module functions, and those functions' doc comments, are unchanged. Zero drift.
