## What changed

Michael's pacing for a hanging and the night's kill (Nov 22). All the wording is his, approved as written. The flow is mine; the art and motion are Limner's: `KillWheel` and `ExecutionMark` in `ui/fx/`, #530.

- **`ui/pacing/pacing_plans.gd`** builds the beats from the world and changes nothing.
  - **A hanging:** fade to black, the condemned's portrait, "<name> was condemned and hanged at dusk...", a beat, the portrait greys and the blue X smashes down, a beat, "... but evil still lurks in Hollowmere.", Continue.
  - **A Martyr:** "<Martyr> died at the gallows in <saved>'s place...", over the Martyr's own portrait.
  - **The night:** fade, then the night's result in a panel, held for Continue. Then "A Demon stalks Hollowmere in the night..." in red and the wheel of everyone alive at nightfall, landing on the victim with the stab, shake and red X. Then "... and <name> met a grim end at the hand of the fiend.", with the name in their colour.
  - **A second Demon kill:** "...and the fiend was not finished." and a new wheel without the first victim, spinning on from where the first stopped.
  - **No Demon kill:** the fakeout, with "... but by mettle or miracle, no one died at the hand of the fiend." in blue. It lands on the warded door only if the ward actually saved someone that night (`w.ward_saves`), otherwise on a person drawn on its own generator (case seed and day), never the world's.
- **`ui/pacing/pacing_overlay.gd`** plays the beats.
  - **Clicks:** a click or Space lands what's moving or ends a pause. Continue goes on. Esc or Skip ends the whole sequence; the log already holds every line.
  - **The shake:** the screen shakes on the wheel's `struck` and on the mark.
- **`ui/main.gd`:** `_consume_headlines` builds the plan when an execution or martyr headline comes in, or a dawn after the first night. It drops the execution, martyr, death and quiet headlines it replaces, and plays the other headlines after the sequence ends. At a game's end, the grim reveal waits for it.
  - **Codex's fixes on review:**
    - A kill that ends the game still plays, though no dawn comes.
    - The hanging waits until the condemned's last words have finished on the courtroom stage.
    - Each night and hanging plays once per case, so a morning `look` doesn't replay it.
    - Headlines already on screen are hidden while it plays, and come back afterwards.
    - A click on the result panel, portrait or wheel goes on, like a click anywhere else.
  - **`pacing_enabled`:** a flag, on by default. `test_ui` turns it off, because its real mouse input after a night otherwise lands on the overlay.

## Edge cases (my plan, which Seneschal accepted)

- **No hanging:** no sequence.
- **A Breathstealer, the Revenant's curse, or another non-Demon death:** these keep their usual headlines, after the sequence. Only Demon kills spin the wheel.
- **The player:** never a target in the sim today, so there's nothing to show.

## Small calls made on Michael's behalf

- Reduced motion halves the beats, as well as dropping the shake, flash and spin (Seneschal relayed it as a small call).
- Each part ends on a Continue button, so the player reads the last line before the town comes back.
