  starting script, automatic joining, vetoes), and my suggestions on unlock order.

- **Perks:** Michael's 11 pitches with notes, including his clarification of Hindsight, and my 6.
- **Other rewards:** challenges, choosing the script, a codex, larger towns, character depth,
  cosmetics, the case archive and fixed cases.
- **Open questions** and who would build what.
