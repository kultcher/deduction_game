# 2026-11-18, Claude-Herald: why playtest 16010's replay drifts (`claude/herald-replay-ints`)

- **Base:** main `4ec65e1`. **Status:** PR open, tools only, zero drift. **Next actor:** Bailiff for the PR; Lantern for the open question below.
- **Source:** Seneschal's investigation request, from Bailiff's question in `docs/playtests/16010.md`. The question was whether Workshop activity during play doesn't reach the log, which would make the replay file 16 fewer cases.

## What I found (replayed at the played build, `832bd17`)

1. **No Workshop action is missing from the log.** The game filed four cases (#45, #88, #95, #136), and all four are logged and replayed. The 16-card gap on day 3 is not missing filings: the replay simply has a different day 3.
   - In the replay the Demon kills **Dorian** on the second night, not Edda. So the day-3 morning, the crime scene and the search all differ, and the board grows more slowly.
   - Your day-3 case therefore lands at #120 instead of #136.
2. **The first real difference comes when the town hears your case against Isolde** (card #88, day 2, the vote that put her on the block).
   - Every logged event before it matches, in type, order, text and numbers. So do the belief snapshots (to two decimals) and the count of each event type.
   - Then **Nisha answers differently.** In play she said she rejected it; in the replay she half-accepted it.
   - Her verdict on the case itself is the same both times. What differs is how far her suspicion of Isolde moved, and that sets what she says.
   - Lian, evil and weighing whether to break cover, then sees a different risk (0.25 in play against 0.50 in the replay). After that, beliefs drift. Dorian's marginal yes on Isolde (0.57 against 0.54 in play) becomes a no, and that night's victim changes.
3. **What doesn't explain it:**
   - Turning the belief cache off, or verifying it, gives the same replay result, with no wrong cache hits.
   - Running the Workshop's Test juror on the case before filing changes nothing.
   - Neither UI testing tool (the "withdraw for a listener" button, the comparison window's retract) was used: no "endorsement withdrawn" refresh appears in the log.
4. **A replay bug I fixed on the way (this PR).** The replay reads a filed case back from JSON, where every number is a float. The argument's key hashes the case, so "version 29" and "29.0" gave every listener a different key in the replay. `tools/log_replay.gd` now turns whole-number floats back into ints before filing. With that fix, the keys match play exactly.
   - On its own the fix doesn't cure 16010: Nisha's answer still differs.
