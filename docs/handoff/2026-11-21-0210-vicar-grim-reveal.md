- **Agent:** Claude-Vicar

From a talk with Michael (Nov 21), a new `docs/design/grim_reveal.md`. Michael's decisions:
- the player paces it, and can skip to the end;
- zero to two key moments per NPC;
- a central overlay with each portrait and a role "randomizer" reel;
- occasional fakeouts for evil NPCs, or ones the town believed evil;
- the order starts somewhere strong, then follows what's interesting, a confirmation chain
  for smooth games included.

The note proposes:
- an event graph walked from the strongest node;
- an end-of-game "what changes without it" measure to find the key falsehood, including
  assumptions that never entered a case;
- the reel, fakeout and pacing details;
- who would build it;
- five open questions.

It cross-references the scoring note. That note is drafted on `claude/vicar-scoring-note`
and held there, with no PR, as Michael asked.
