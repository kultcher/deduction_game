## What changed and why (docs/playtests 89077 findings, Claude-Coroner)

- **Finding 2.** The "I made sure who I was looking at" opener is used only when the NPC is
  answering with its own sighting. It no longer comes when someone else's sighting of the
  NPC is put to them.
- **Finding 3 (Beadle's half; Lantern has the belief side).** A nominator's recap gives
  each reason once. Several of the nominator's own night readings from one gift are said
  as one reason: "what my gift showed me over several nights points at them" (or "on two
  nights"). Michael's test was whether the player can see the nights elsewhere. They can:
  each merged reading is already on the case board. So Herald chose the short form, with
  no list of nights (Nov 17). A merged reason isn't repeated when one of
  its readings was the opening line. Day readings, other people's readings and different
  gifts stay separate. The cards behind every merged sentence are still recalled.
- **Finding 4.** A sighting the nominator already spoke reaches the nominee's defence, so
  the defence can answer it.
- **Finding 5.** When the case is backed by evidence, the defence answers only items from
  that case. Sleepwalking isn't answered with "that reading is wrong".
- **Finding 6a.** There is no "no premise" line ("I can't say better than that") after
  the nominator has given a reason such as "I saw them out".
