Doesn't mean it's wrong.", or "Those accounts clash...") went on to `present_accept_open`, "Fits what I know...", when the case still moved the listener. It now goes on to a new line, `present_clashes_moved` (Bart: "Still changes how I see {target}."). There is one generic line per voice, not tied to a gift or a card. A partial stance keeps `present_partial`, which doesn't contradict the opener.

Both are in all 15 voices, with fallbacks "{source} is lying, and you should ask yourselves why." and "{source} lied, and you should ask yourselves why." Nothing calls them yet; Beadle wires them, and has the keys and fallbacks.
