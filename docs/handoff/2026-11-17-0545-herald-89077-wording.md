## What changed

1. **The Warden says "the first night", not "Night 1"** (review item 5). `answer_gift_custom` used the card-text night name in speech. It now uses the spoken one ("the first night at Odette's door; last night at Dorian's door"). The list follows a colon in every voice, so its first letter is no longer capitalised.
2. **The Medium did the same** when relaying a sighting or a guard ("...out on the Docks on night 0", "...on night 2"). These readings are kept and retold on later days, so they use a fixed ordinal ("the night before you arrived", "on the second night") rather than "last night".
3. **The Registrar's reading** started "My register lists three roles", so after a night phrase it came out as "The night before you arrived, My register lists...". It's now "my register listed three roles: ...", lower-case and past tense like the other readings. Retelling it ("their register") now works too; the capital "My" had stopped that.
4. **A first pressed home alibi** (review item 2, the line's assumption; Beadle has the routing). Pressed with a sighting before ever giving an account of that night, the speaker now says the plain "I was at home {when}." rather than "I stand by that account" or a voice's "my account is unchanged". Repeat presses keep today's lines.
