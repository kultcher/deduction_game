- **Base:** main `babb7d6` (first opened on `c676018`). **Status:** PR open, re-baselined. **Next actor:** Bailiff. This PR moves golden, so it merges on its own.
- **Source:** Coroner's review of playtest 89077 (PR #200, `docs/playtests/89077.md`), relayed by Seneschal.

## What changed

1. **The Warden says "the first night", not "Night 1"** (review item 5). `answer_gift_custom` used the card-text night name in speech. It now uses the spoken one ("the first night at Odette's door; last night at Dorian's door"). The list follows a colon in every voice, so its first letter is no longer capitalised.
2. **The Medium did the same** when relaying a sighting or a guard ("...out on the Docks on night 0", "...on night 2"). These readings are kept and retold on later days, so they use a fixed ordinal ("the night before you arrived", "on the second night") rather than "last night".
3. **The Registrar's reading** started "My register lists three roles", so after a night phrase it came out as "The night before you arrived, My register lists...". It's now "my register listed three roles: ...", lower-case and past tense like the other readings. Retelling it ("their register") now works too; the capital "My" had stopped that.
4. **A first pressed home alibi** (review item 2, the line's assumption; Beadle has the routing). Pressed with a sighting before ever giving an account of that night, the speaker gives their own voice's alibi, as asking about the night would (Michael, Nov 17), rather than "I stand by that account" or a voice's "my account is unchanged". Repeat presses keep today's lines.
5. **The count line** (review item 3; Michael, Nov 17). A Lamplighter's count names nobody, so as a reason it said "my reading, taken with the other reports, leaves them among the likeliest". It now says "my count of folk abroad that night doesn't square with what we've been told". Retold by someone else: "the Lamplighter's count of..." or "Agnes's count of...". That third-person form is my small call. It applies to any gift that counts walkers (`count_mode == "walkers"`), not to the Lamplighter by name.
6. **A home alibi for an earlier night** read "Home. All on night 2." in some voices: the voices' alibis say "all last night", and an earlier night was swapped in as "on night 2". It's now the spoken ordinal ("all the second night").
7. **Scholarly words** (Michael, Nov 17): `docs/DIALOGUE_DESIGN.md` gets principle 9, "learned, not modern or academic", with a pointer from Hollis's and Nisha's entries. Existing lines aren't reworded; it applies to new dialogue.
