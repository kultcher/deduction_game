## What changed

- **`ui/board_view.gd`: a card at reading zoom (option B).**
  - **Taller card:** 78 world units instead of 58.
  - **Gutter:** the kind icon and the role or place icon stack in a gutter down the right, instead of sharing one corner.
  - **Badges as tabs** along the top edge: "private" (told to you privately), "in confidence" (you promised to keep it quiet) and "context" (background that can't establish guilt alone). The data already existed (`CardModel.badges`, `EvidenceContext.numbers`), but nothing drew it.
  - **Text** wraps to the lines that fit, and cut text ends in "…". Laid-out paragraphs are cached.
  - **"Heard" line:** shown only at the detail zoom, on its own line, ending in "…" when cut. The hover tooltip still has it at every zoom.
  - **Overview zoom:** the chips are unchanged.
- **`game/card_model.gd`: shorter normal-zoom wording** for kinds that used to be cut off at 57 characters:
  - accusations ("Kiran accused Greta");
  - opinions ("Isolde vouches for Merrin");
  - alibis ("Merrin: home, the Market");
  - sightings ("Kiran working at the bakery");
  - conversations ("Kiran and Hollis talking at the town well");
  - deaths ("Edda killed, the Market");
  - executions ("Hollis executed");
  - quiet nights ("Nobody died").

  The time is left out because the column shows it. Full sentences still show at the detail zoom and in the tooltip.
- **`docs/DIALOGUE_DESIGN.md` §7.6:** the standing rule for board and card text.

## Small calls made on Michael's behalf

- **Badge colours:** "private" is muted blue, "in confidence" is the theme's red, and "context" is grey-brown.
- **Kinds without a short form** (hints, secrets, votes, gift readings) keep their existing short text. Gift readings already have one per role. Hints and secrets still cut at 57 characters, which I left for a later pass.
- **Detail-zoom text:** the full sentences, generated by the simulation and game code, aren't reworded here. "Kiran publicly accused Greta." is still what you read zoomed in. Trimming those is a wider change across several files, so I propose it as a follow-up.
