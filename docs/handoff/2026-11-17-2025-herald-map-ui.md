# 2026-11-17, Claude-Herald: the map in a conversation or meeting, and Workshop panning (`claude/herald-map-ui`)

- **Base:** main `f754421`. **Status:** PR open. **Next actor:** Bailiff.
- **Source:** Michael's three UI requests (Nov 17, through Seneschal), BACKLOG [decided], Herald's. All three are built here and moved to BACKLOG_COMPLETED.md.

## What changed

1. **The conversation's options wait while the map is up.** In a conversation, "Look at the map" used to move the options into the lower pane, so the player could carry on with the map open. Now only "Look at conversation" is offered there. The options come back with the conversation view. (`ui/main.gd`, the "talk" actions.)
2. **"Return to conversation" / "Return to meeting" at the map's top right.** The button (Michael's wording) shows whenever the map has replaced the conversation or the meeting circle, and it brings that view back. The lower-pane toggles are still there. (`ui/main.gd`: `map_return`, `_on_map_return`.)
3. **Workshop: left-drag on empty space pans again**, and a plain click there clears the selection. Shift+left-drag draws the selection box. Middle and right drag still pan. The canvas hint now reads "...drag empty space to pan · shift-drag to select several...". (`ui/workshop_canvas_view.gd`, `ui/case_workshop_canvas.gd`.)

## Not changed

- The meeting's own actions stay in the lower pane while its map is up. Michael asked to hide the options for the conversation only.

## Tests

- New `tools/test_map_return.gd`. In a conversation: the options show; on the map, none are offered and the return button shows; pressing it brings the conversation and its options back. The same for the meeting circle.
- New `tools/test_workshop_pan.gd`. A left-press on empty space pans and clears the selection, and a drag moves the view. A shift+left-press starts a box that doesn't move the view, and releasing selects the cards inside it.
- These are UI suites, so they aren't in the CI fast list; main runs them.
- The results of the related UI suites and the golden check are in the PR body.
