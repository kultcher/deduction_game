## What changed

Coroner's 52102 findings in my area (`docs/playtests/52102.md`; routed Nov 27).

1. **The summary reads the daily guess window.** The window keeps its own picks and never writes to the profile, which is Michael's design. But the case summary, the outcome text and the "private theory" line read only the profile, so a player who used the window got "not recorded".
   - **The window's verdict:** `GameScreens.effective_guesses(g)` is the window's last locked picks when there are any, and the profile's guesses otherwise. `capture_prediction` and `theory_status` use it.
   - **The conversion:** `CaseMetrics.window_guesses` turns the picks into the profile's shape. The Demon circle comes first, then the Minion circles.
   - **Untyped picks:** they take a stand-in type of their side, because the summary judges who, not which type. The theory line shows an untyped Minion as plain "Minion".
   - **Not touched:** the bots never open the window, so their runs are unchanged.
2. **The townsfolk list freezes the old phase's lines.** `cast_list.prime()` notes the lines just before each command (`_run` and `_run_workshop`). When a command ends the phase and the screen holds, `freeze()` uses those lines instead of reading the world after it moved on. `test_control_flow` now checks the lines themselves, not just that the list is frozen.
3. **`grimoire lies` dates a reading by the night it reads.** A fabrication held back and told a night later still reads its own night, e.g. Dorian's night-1 lie as N1, not N2. A day-phase reading reads as D<n>. Beadle has the other half: planted sightings never logged as lies.
4. **Unresolved, now traceable:** at seq 558 something recorded a Demon guess that no command made. A guess from the profile now logs where it came from in its `player_guess` revision (`source`: `profile_menu` or `profile_save`). A typed guess or a bot's carries none, so golden is unchanged. The next playtest's log will say whether the player clicked it.
5. **A silenced person isn't offered questions** (seq 747). The conversation buttons drop the asks, with the hint "X can't speak until tomorrow evening.". The typed options read "(silenced: present … | offer ward|vigil (free) | bye)". Asking was already refused.
6. **`guess X minion`** now says how to name a Minion: "Name the Minion's gift ('guess x poisoner'), or guess 'evil'." Accepting a plain "Minion" guess would be a new guess value, which is Michael's call.
- **Already fixed on main:** "Oh, Last night" (seq 890). 52102 was played at f6f0ae58, which predates e2d39bdf, the 51795 splice fix.
