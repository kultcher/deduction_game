## C. Design rules

- **No bespoke per-role interactions:** nothing new breaks this. The role-specific changes
  (Dowser bluff, Medium duty, Warden night 0) are each role's own gift rules. The kill-score
  change keys off the general `first_night` flag, and the deal-before-claim hold off
  `request_day`, for anyone.
- **Dialogue vocabulary:** no new NPC line uses "data", "variable" or "constraint". The new
  Workshop notes ("No new info: …", "Refreshes your day-%d argument …") are player-facing
  UI, and "No new info" is the name Michael gave the note (triage 5.2). Not flagged.
- **Night ordinals:** no new spoken reference to a numbered night.
- **C# baseline:** the Warden night-0 rule went into the C# kernel (`LiveActivity.cs`) and
  the GDScript mirror together. No parity work suggested.
- **"Claim":** the test juror now hears the public claims of the people a case names
  (`WorkshopCanvas.public_claims_for`). It filters on `kind == "claim"` cards whose source is
  their subject, which means role claims only. That is the right scope for "who claimed
  what gift", and it doesn't redefine "claim".
