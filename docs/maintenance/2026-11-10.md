## Needs Michael

1. **Some new NPC lines break the "no modern words" rule.** The new voice-pack lines say
   "Okay, …", "Yep, … Here's the thing, though", "I dunno if this helps", "Its relevance is
   uncertain" and "Possibly irrelevant". They come from #115 (owning a true sighting) and
   #117 (asking about the dead). Easy to hand to a Claude session; exact lines in section C.
2. **A design question from #117: asking about someone who has died now leaves out what
   people saw.** Before, asking about the morning's victim could get "I saw them at the
   tavern with X last evening", which is the most useful thing to learn about a fresh
   victim. Now the answer is only "they're gone", plus maybe their old haunts. If that was
   on purpose, nothing to do; if not, it's a one-line fix (section A, item 3).
3. **Bugs to hand to a Claude session** (section A, items 1, 2, 4, 5). In plain terms:
   in the full-screen Workshop, the Trace Solver and Whereabouts panels open *behind* it,
   so the buttons look dead; "Undo remove" after starting or loading another case can paste
   the old case's cards into the new one; the jury preview in the Workshop can see your raw
   day watches, so it rates silencing and Hexer cases higher than a real listener would; and
   a townsperson who admits "I was out that night" can then refuse to say why, and blame you
   for asking even when an NPC nominated them.
4. **Two design questions, no rush.** (a) The Muddler's Gossip is now forced to at least 0.7.
   A player who knows the cast could spot the Muddler from one "they're a gossip" remark
   about a normally quiet character. (b) "Hexer" is now the day-killing demon, but
   BACKLOG.md and DESIGN.md still list a planned *minion* called Hexer ("two cursed
   townsfolk"). One of them may want a new name.
5. **No fresh win rates for this window.** The only sweep since the last report ran on
   `61ba493`, which is older than everything reviewed tonight: bot 59.0% (7 NPCs) and 53.7%
   (10 NPCs), with no passive cells. Nothing measured the Hexer, one shift each, or the day-1
   gift change. A sweep of `b40fab1` (give the full 40-character id or the branch name
   `main`) with passive cells would cover it. Last report's open bugs (2026-11-09, A1–A4)
   are also still in the code.
