## A. Code review

Ranked by severity. None of these affects play today. Two are in the connections model,
which is off by default (`Tuning.Workshop.CONNECTIONS = false`).

- **A1 (low, model off): a "suspicious" case's pressure fades faster than the case.**
  `workshop/connections.gd:867` computes `withheld` with `case_withheld(..., fade)`, the flat
  0.5 a day, while the counted half two lines earlier uses `rate`, the buy-in rate (up to
  0.9). Failure: a listener who bought a suspicious case on day 1 still counts it at about
  0.9 of its weight on day 2, but the pressure on its subject
  (`game/workshop_presentation.gd` `_connections_rise`) is computed from a held-back half at
  0.5, so the subject is pressed to explain about half as hard as the case's standing
  implies. Open PR #155 removes `withheld` entirely, which fixes this. If #155 doesn't land,
  the fix is to pass `rate` there.
- **A2 (informational): re-presenting reorders presentations with the model off as well.**
  `game/workshop_presentation.gd:118` (`proposals.erase(key)` before re-inserting) runs
  whatever the switch, though the comment gives a connections-only reason. Golden and the
  suites are identical, so nothing reads that order today with the model off. I'm noting it
  in case a later change starts depending on presentation order.
- **A3 (informational, tools only): `Connections.enabled` stays on after a replay.**
  `tools/log_replay.gd:33` turns the static switch on for a log recorded with the model on
  and never turns it off. A tool that replays several logs in one process would replay the
  later ones with the model on. Today's callers replay one log per process.

## C. Design rules

- **No bespoke per-role interactions:** the Breathstealer's hunt is its own role behaviour,
  and `DayPresence.hunt_block` models the silencing mechanic the same way `HEX_SEEK_SHARE`
  models the Hexer. Neither is NPC logic keyed on a specific role. One borderline case:
  `workshop/reason_attribution.gd:907` words a Warden reading specially ("I guarded X that
  night, and their account of that night doesn't fit it"). It only changes the spoken reason,
  not the weighing, so I'm not treating it as a violation.
- **Vocabulary:** the window's new dialogue avoids modern terms. It actually removes some
  ("premises" becomes "accounts" or "reports", "You are missing a premise" becomes "For the
  record"). The Why report strings that say "connections model" are a developer tool, not
  dialogue.
- **Night ordinals:** the new defence line uses `C.spoken_when`. "Day N" appears only on
  card, totem-fact and grimoire text, not in speech.
- **C# baseline:** no GDScript-parity suggestions are made, and none are needed (see A).
- **"Claim":** used consistently in the new reason wording ("their claim to be the …").
