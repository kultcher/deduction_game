## A. Code review

Ranked most severe first. All from reading unless marked.

1. **Impossible public record makes every fabrication "self-incriminating" (medium).**
   `sim/fabrication_check.gd:95-98`: when the record *before* the lie already has no
   possible world, `assess` returns `lift 1.0, impossible: true`. That says nothing about
   the new lie. `vet` then rejects every alternative against the same baseline, and
   `sim/evil.gd:752-773` re-holds the reading each pass while the record stays
   impossible. An evil member then stops telling readings at all. Suggested fix: an empty
   `before` means "can't judge", so the lie is let through. Owner: Beadle.
2. **Reveal logs the held version, not the told one (medium).** `sim/evil.gd:812` logs
   `fake_info` with the original text when a reading is held. On release
   (`sim/evil.gd:768-773`) a reworked reading goes into `info_log` with only a `think`
   line, without its new text or its reason. `game_screens.gd` builds the end-of-game lie
   list from `fake_info`, so it shows a lie nobody heard and omits the one they did. This
   undercuts the grimoire payoff and "every lie has a logged reason". Owner: Beadle.
3. **Lie reasons replaced by raw ids (low-medium, wording).** The `why` strings from
   `bluff_alternatives` use raw ids or `str(array)`: `sim/roles/archivist.gd:334`,
   `clerk.gd:371`, `rumormonger.gd:573`, `seer.gd:604`, `executor.gd:412`,
   `gravedigger.gd:442`. They replace the motive at `sim/evil.gd:808`, and
   `World.false_reading_reason` prints them in the reveal. Owners: Beadle (code), Herald
   (wording, for Michael).
4. **Several doubted readings in one judgement: doubt is under-applied, order-dependent
   (medium).** `workshop/case_eval.gd:1192-1213` (#186). Each doubted card is mixed
   against an alternative built from the original cards with only that one swapped, so
   the earlier cards' doubt gets diluted. A false Night Watch with both readings fully
   voided (`honest = 1.0`) ends up with the first reading at full trust. A source-wide
   doubt ("src:*") applies fully only to the last card in order. Owner: Lantern.
5. **A day sighting still marks the same-numbered night as "in the case" (low-medium).**
   `sim/mind_meeting.gd:1629-1630`: the night-form id goes into `case_eids` before the
   `by_day` switch, against the comment at 1631-1634. A case citing the accuser's day-1
   sighting can still let the defence answer with "where I was last night", the Isolde
   (26532) bug the change set out to fix. Owner: Beadle.
6. **Hand-kept card-kind lists inside the CaseEval cache (architecture, #181, Argon).**
   `workshop/case_eval.gd:1171-1177` (`_is_role_card`, `_is_movement_role_card`), used at

8. **Floors and stated reasons come from different cards (low-medium, #187).**

## C. Design rules

- **Per-role special cases:** the new `RoleDef` hooks (`bluff_alternatives`,
  `duty_explains_outing`, `bluff_duty_covers`, `night_result_in_sightings`) are general
  hooks with per-role data, which is fine. The hard-coded role lists in
  `fabrication_check.gd` aren't (A10). The Lovers rule is filtered in GDScript in
  `case_eval.gd` and `solver.gd`, not in the C# kernel (from #162). That isn't a solver
  fallback, but it's split across two files.
- **NPCs as perfect solvers (for Vicar/Michael):** `FabricationCheck.vet` audits each lie
  with the full neutral-listener judge and picks the alternative that most raises
  suspicion on the scapegoat. Insight decides whether the liar notices, but once it does,
  its choice is exact. The double-claim pairing (A9) leans the same way.
- **Modern words and night numbers in spoken text:** none in the new lines. Night
  numbers appear only in debug `think` lines and older card text. Wording for Michael:
  - `game/workshop_canvas.gd:126` "Adapter available · ..." is a code term on a Workshop
    card. The prefix is older than this window.
  - `game/workshop_why.gd:89-90` "Every Confessor claim, read together" treats "claim" as
    role claim only. Draft: "Everyone who claims the Confessor's gift, read together".
- **Determinism:** no unseeded randomness found. New draws use `w.rng` and sorted or
  insertion-ordered iteration.
- **Sim separate from presentation:** nothing under `sim/` references `ui/` or `game/`.
