## A. Code review

Ranked by severity. Each one comes from reading the code and was not reproduced in the game.

1. **Medium. A late relay names the night twice, once as a number, for Veteran and Medium
   readings.** `sim/mind_meeting.gd:253` now puts `C.spoken_when(day)` in front of every relayed
   reading's text. That fits the Monitor, Confessor and Sexton texts, which no longer carry a time
   (yesterday's fix). Other role texts still have one built in:
   - `sim/roles/veteran.gd:43`: "the demon came for me on night %d…"
   - `sim/roles/medium.gd:132`: "…saw %s out on %s on night %d"
   - `sim/roles/medium.gd:139`: "…home on their last night"

   *Scenario:* a Veteran survives the attack on night 3 and is killed later. At the next meeting a
   friend says: "Before Hugh died, they told me they were the Veteran, and that last night the
   demon came for them on night 3 and they lived…". That says when twice and breaks the ordinal
   rule. *Suggested fix:* store the night in `data` for these texts, as was done for the Monitor
   and Confessor. Or strip a trailing time phrase the way `_info_intent` does
   (`sim/mind_meeting.gd:342`). Whichever is chosen, add the Veteran to `test_codex_fixes`'
   late-relay check.

2. **Medium-low. The preference gate compares a ledger weight against an agreement threshold.**
   `sim/mind.gd:936` skips a presentation-backed `arg:` entry when
   `e.weight < PREMISE_PREF_MIN_AGREEMENT` (0.5). That weight is not the agreement: it is
   `agreement × venue scale × (0.5 + trust)` (`sim/presentation_reply.gd:199`, then `_record`).
   The presented-premise gate a few lines below does read the agreement
   (`presentation_agreement`).

   *Scenario:* at a meeting (scale 0.6), a listener who fully accepts a cited defence
   (agreement 1.0) while their trust in the player is 0.3 stores 1.0 × 0.6 × 0.8 = 0.48. That never
   sets a protect preference. In private, at trust 0.8, an argument at agreement 0.39 gets through
   (0.39 × 1.0 × 1.3 = 0.51). *Suggested fix:* store the agreement in the entry's `meta` and gate on
   that.

3. **Low. A "readings don't fit together" premise overwrites any other challenge on its first
   reading.** `workshop/live_assessment.gd:210` writes the group challenge into
   `reading_challenges[group[0]]`. That replaces a "Suspected lie" or "Failed gift" the player put
   on that card in the canvas. A later `lied`/`misled` premise on the same card replaces the group
   challenge in turn (line 215). It also writes into the root case's challenges when the premise
   is a sub-argument. *Scenario:* the player marks reading A as a suspected lie and also argues
   "X's readings A and B don't fit together". A's lie challenge is silently dropped. *Suggested
   fix:* key group challenges separately, for example under `"group:" + target`.

4. **Low. Poisoner candidates for a group read only the first reading's night.**
   `workshop/reading_challenges.gd:275` (`poisoner_candidates`) takes the night from
   `report`, which is the group's first card. In a "failed gift" group of readings from different
   nights, people seen out on the other nights are never candidates.
