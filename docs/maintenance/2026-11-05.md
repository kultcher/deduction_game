1. **High: the Sexton's bell doesn't stop the Breathstealer.** `sim/roles/breathstealer.gd:60`
   and `sim/world.gd:849` never call `is_minion_suppressed()`. The comment at `sim/world.gd:1300`,

   `tools/test_chunk6a_sexton_brute.gd` doesn't test this.
2. **High: an honest Sexton lies about the toll night.** The bell walk puts them out of doors,
   but `sim/roles/sexton.gd` has `night_walk = false` and no `uses_night_duty_alibi`. So
   `answer_night` (`sim/mind_dialogue.gd:813-838`) treats them as hiding something. With no
   secret to confess, they claim "home all night" (recorded as a lie) or refuse. The podium does
   the same (`sim/mind_podium.gd:168`). Scenario: someone sees the Sexton from a window, the
   Sexton denies being out, and good looks evil. Also, their reading is added with `quiet=true`
   (`sim/roles/sexton.gd:50`), which marks it shared, so meeting testimony and gossip skip it.
   This is separate from the known solver gap on the Sexton's route.
3. **High: an evil Monitor bluffer reports all their sightings.** `override_night_sightings`
   (`sim/roles/monitor.gd:162-170`) ignores the night asked about and replaces the filtered list
   built at `sim/mind_dialogue.gd:856-870`. That drops the teammate and vantage checks, and at
   the podium it bypasses `_public_sighting_allowed`. Scenario: asked about last night, a Demon
   claiming Monitor names everyone seen all case, including the Poisoner, as seen that night.

   `autoplay.gd -- --seed 101032 --npcs 10 --passive --dynamic`.
5. **Medium: a script with no spare town role breaks the case.** `sim/script_def.gd:188` only
