# Voice calibration: first writing pass (Polished & Heightened)

Drafts for editorial review, following [DIALOGUE_DESIGN](DIALOGUE_DESIGN.md).
Four short situations per speaker, plus bonus stress/accusation sketches where noted.
Because the shared text formatter highlights names, places, and roles in bold/color,
voices with larger syntactic footprints (Fenwick, Hollis, Constance, Dorian, Merrin,
Nisha, Kiran) are encouraged to take up space rather than compress into generic brevity.

## Twelve draft voices

### Dorian — The Apologetic Overthinker

Worries about how his words sound and recursively audits his own phrasing, without becoming uncertain of the observation itself.

- **Gift refusal:** “I'd rather not say—which immediately sounds evasive, doesn't it? Sorry, I'm not trying to be difficult, I'm just... still not saying.”
- **Routine alibi:** “I was at home all last night. Alone, obviously, which means I can't prove it—not that I'm assuming you think I'm lying, just... I was in bed. All night.”
- **Firsthand report:** “I saw Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening—I mean, I happened to be there, and so were they, not that I was keeping tabs on them or anything! I just saw them.”
- **The same report under doubt:** “Wait—have I explained that badly? Because I did see Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening. No, I said that clearly enough the first time. I stand by it.”

### Edda — The Unflappable Earth Mother

Grounds the exchange in warm, unbothered practicality; cozy rhythm without inventing props or folk proverbs on every turn.

- **Gift refusal:** “I'll be keeping my gift to myself for now, if you don't mind. Plenty of other things we can talk over.”
- **Routine alibi:** “Home all last night, snug as a bug and quiet as you please.”
- **Firsthand report:** “Oh, Rowan? Saw them at the tavern yesterday evening, plain as day.”
- **The same report under doubt:** “Well, fretting at the answer won't change what these eyes saw, dear. Rowan was at the tavern yesterday evening. Same question, same answer.”

### Fenwick — The Incorrigible Raconteur

Delights in staging the reveal and addressing an audience; uses character-specific flourishes rather than fabricating routes or actions.

- **Gift refusal:** “Ah, you'd have me spoil the third act in the prologue! My gift stays with me, my friend.”
- **Routine alibi:** “My grand adventure last night? Home, the whole night through. A tragedy for the bards, I grant you, but every hero needs an off-evening.”
- **Firsthand report:** “Picture the scene: yesterday evening at the tavern, and who should grace my line of sight? None other than Rowan—clear as the nose on your face!” *(In live play, appends target-specific epithet; see table below).*
- **The same report under doubt:** “Ah, you wound me to the quick! Criticize the performance if you must, my friend, but the fact remains: Rowan was at the tavern yesterday evening, and I saw them myself.”
- **Serious mode (Grief / Tragedy):** “Rowan was at the tavern yesterday evening. That's all. I don't have a story for it today.”

### Greta — The Suspicious Gatekeeper

Treats every question as an incursion; tests the questioner's entitlement and demands receipts rather than offering efficiency.

- **Gift refusal:** “And why would I hand that knowledge over to you? My gift stays my business.”
- **Routine alibi:** “Under my own roof all last night. I'm a civilized woman, not some rapscallion prowling the lanes at all hours.”
- **Firsthand report:** “I saw Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening. Make of it what you want, if you're keeping a tally.”
- **The same report under doubt:** “Rowan was at the tavern yesterday evening, just as I said. You think my eyes are going? Show me who puts them elsewhere, then. Until you do, that's where they were.”

### Hollis — The Exacting Scholar

Weighs definitions, scope, and epistemic categories; pedantically qualifies *how* something is known without inventing finer clock times or sub-locations.

- **Gift refusal:** “A perfectly lucid and reasonable inquiry—which I shall, upon reflection, decline to answer. My gift remains my own affair.”
- **Routine alibi:** “At home for the entirety of last night. 'Entirety' being the operative term, mind you—not a partial sojourn. Do record that accurately.”
- **Firsthand report:** “I observed Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening—'observed' in the strict visual sense, you understand, as opposed to meresecondhand inference.”
- **The same report under doubt:** “I observed Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening. Your skepticism is a fascinating rhetorical posture, of course, but hardly a sufficient ground for revising an empirical fact.”
- **Accusation response:** “A novel hypothesis. Entirely unsupported by the sequence of events, mind you, but novel nonetheless.”

### Isolde — The Frightened Peacemaker

Fears the human cost of testimony; cushions the interpersonal blow and pleads for fairness while keeping the observation itself intact.

- **Gift refusal:** “I'm sorry, truly—I'd much rather keep my gift to myself, if you can forgive me that.”
- **Routine alibi:** “I stayed home all last night. Please take my word for it.”
- **Firsthand report:** “I did see Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening... though I'd hate for a simple sighting to bring them any trouble.”
- **The same report under doubt:** “I'm not trying to stir up strife, truly! I saw Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening—please don't ask me to recant what I know I saw.”

### Jory — The Restless Enthusiast

Speaks in kinetic, telegraphic bursts; races ahead to the next useful question and gets flustered when forced to repeat himself.

- **Gift refusal:** “Not saying what my gift is. Something else? Come on, ask!”
- **Routine alibi:** “Home. All last night, start to finish. Didn't step out, didn't see a soul—wait, did something happen on my block?”
- **Firsthand report:** “Rowan. Tavern, yesterday evening. Saw them myself. Anything else?”
- **The same report under doubt:** “What? No, I saw Rowan! Tavern. Yesterday evening. Which bit of that do you want me to say again?”

### Kiran — The Smiling Deflector

Treats interrogation as a parlor game; turns the spotlight back onto the questioner's tactics with effortless irony.

- **Gift refusal:** “Asking for my cards on the very first turn, Demonologist? Half the charm of a parlor game is the guessing. I'll keep my gift to myself.”
- **Routine alibi:** “Home all last night, I'm afraid. You were hoping for a much more scandalous story, weren't you?”
- **Firsthand report:** “Rowan was at the tavern yesterday evening—I saw them myself. There now, a question I can actually oblige you on. Don't spend it all in one place.”
- **The same report under doubt:** “Come now, you asked for my account—nobody said it had to pass an audition. I saw Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening.”
- **Serious mode (Cornered / Accused):** “I told you where I was. If you're going to hang me, get on with it; if not, stop circling.”

### Lian — The No-Nonsense Problem Solver

Approaches conversation as crisis triage; strips social padding and demands to know how a question advances the investigation.

- **Gift refusal:** “Naming my gift won't catch a killer today. Next question.”
- **Routine alibi:** “Home all last night. Cross it off and tell me what else needs checking.”
- **Firsthand report:** “Rowan was at the tavern yesterday evening. Saw them myself. If that doesn't narrow your list, let's move on.”
- **The same report under doubt:** “Rowan was at the tavern yesterday evening. Staring at me won't change the answer—either check it against someone else or move on.”

### Merrin — The Melodramatic Catastrophist

Frames everyday exchanges as portents of doom before landing squarely on the concrete fact; high theatricality with zero fabricated evidence.

- **Gift refusal:** “Must we drag every private mercy into thepitiless light? No—my gift stays with me, thank you!”
- **Routine alibi:** “Shut inside my home the whole wretched night, thank providence! For all the good four walls do against the dark, I certainly wasn't about to test my luck outside!”
- **Firsthand report:** “Oh, I dread what grim conclusion you'll draw from this, truly I do—but I saw Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening.”
- **The same report under doubt:** “Heaven help us, now my own eyes are put on trial! I saw Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening—would that I hadn't, if this is the thanks I get!”

### Nisha — The Patient Investigator

Treats testimony as a set of discrete, testable variables; isolates what an observation proves versus what remains unverified.

- **Gift refusal:** “I'm not disclosing my gift. Treat it as an unknown variable and work around it for now.”
- **Routine alibi:** “I stayed home all last night. That is my account—though I'm well aware a self-report isn't independent confirmation.”
- **Firsthand report:** “I saw Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening. Log the sighting, by all means, but keep the observation separate from what it implies.”
- **The same report under doubt:** “I saw Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening. If you're rejecting the premise, be specific—which part do you dispute: the person, the place, or the time?”
- **Reasoned withholding (When caller supplies tactical-secrecy rationale):** “If I name my gift now, the Demon adjusts its plan tonight. You don't have enough on the board to make that trade worth it.”

### Odette — The Dry-Witted Independent

Low-energy contempt for theatrics; uses literalism and velvet-edged sarcasm to keep the investigator at arm's length.

- **Gift refusal:** “A fair enough question. I'm sure I'll let you know the moment it becomes your business.”
- **Routine alibi:** “At home all last night, surprisingly enough. I find the night air lacks a certain charm when there's a murderer about.”
- **Firsthand report:** “Rowan was at the tavern yesterday evening. I saw them myself. Utterly scandalous, I'm sure.”
- **The same report under doubt:** “I did see Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening. Did you want my actual recollection, or shall I invent a version that flatters your theory?”

## Established-voice references (Heightened Constance)

### Agnes — The Village Auntie
- **Gift refusal:** “I'm keeping that to myself, love. I hope you'll understand.”
- **Routine alibi:** “I was home all last night, dear. Nothing more to tell you there.”
- **Firsthand report:** “Oh, yes—I did see Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening.”
- **The same report under doubt:** “All right, let's talk it through calmly. I saw Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening. What troubles you about that?”

### Bartholomew — The Stone Wall
- **Gift refusal:** “That's mine to know.”
- **Routine alibi:** “Home. All night.”
- **Firsthand report:** “Saw Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening.”
- **The same report under doubt:** “Rowan. Tavern. Yesterday evening. That's my account.”

### Constance — The Moral Arbiter
- **Gift refusal:** “I shall not disclose a private gift merely because you see fit to demand it.”
- **Routine alibi:** “I remained at home throughout last night, as any person of proper conduct ought.”
- **Firsthand report:** “As I recall, Rowan was present at the tavern yesterday evening. I witnessed them there myself.”
- **The same report under doubt:** “I do not give testimony lightly, Demonologist. I saw Rowan at the tavern yesterday evening; if you mean to impugn my word, state your grounds.”
