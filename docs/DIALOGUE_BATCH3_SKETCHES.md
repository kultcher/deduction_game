# Dialogue Expansion — Third Writing Pass (5 Core Categories × 15 Voices)

Drafts for editorial review and implementation, following `docs/DIALOGUE_DESIGN.md` and `docs/DIALOGUE_VOICE_SKETCHES.md`.

These five categories cover the next tier of **high-frequency generic strings** across private negotiations, pressed night challenges, gift follow-ups, role queries, and town meeting clashes:

1. **Fear-Based Silence & Protection / Confidentiality Bargaining** (`sim/mind_dialogue.gd:365–405, 462`, `game/game.gd:962, 1021, 1044, 1051`)
2. **Pressed Night Challenges, Trapped Lie Admissions & Secret Confessions** (`sim/mind_dialogue.gd:194, 211–217, 636`, `sim/mind_meeting.gd:830`, `game/workshop_presentation.gd:160`)
3. **Gift Follow-Ups: Repeat Confirmations, Passive Gifts, Empty & Withheld Readings** (`sim/mind_dialogue.gd:502, 514, 545, 553, 559`)
4. **Role Queries (`ask about <role>`) & Directing a Gift (`ask check / guard / watch`)** (`sim/social.gd:464, 503`, `sim/mind.gd:995–1003`)
5. **Town Meeting Clashes: Public Accusations, Relays & Gallows Last Words** (`sim/mind_meeting.gd:291, 377, 429, 441, 509, 511`)

All lines obey the strict simulation boundary:

* **Zero alignment or truth leakage:** Honest townsfolk and evil bluffers use the exact same templates when executing the same move.
* **No assumed sequence of events:** Generic refusals, trust-drop withholdings, and gallows lines work cleanly whether triggered by minor question fatigue, a quiet Day 1, or a heated late-game trial.
* **Varied phrasing & register discipline:** Each category varies its core idioms across the cast rather than repeating a single catchphrase, Agnes spaces out her affectionate terms so linked sequences do not stack them, fear refusals orient around waiting until disclosure is most useful rather than waiting for the town to become "safer", and modern analytical terms like `"data"` remain strictly reserved for Nisha.

## Category 1: Fear-Based Silence & Protection / Confidentiality Bargaining

When an NPC is afraid (`_afraid(w, n)`) or cautious about revealing their gift/readings:

* **Fear-based gift refusal (`fear_gift_refusal`):** Replaces `"If I say what I am, I'll be dead by morning. Ask me again when it's safer."` (`sim/mind_dialogue.gd:462`), orienting around holding the reveal until it can do the most good or once they have more to show.
* **Requesting a Ward (`request_ward`):** Replaces `"Put your ward on my door tonight, and I'll tell you what I am. I can't say it otherwise."` (`sim/mind_dialogue.gd:366`).
* **Requesting a Vigil (`request_vigil`, with `{block}`):** Replaces `"Watch over {block} tonight, and I'll tell you what I am. I daren't otherwise."` (`sim/mind_dialogue.gd:367`).
* **Requesting Confidentiality (`request_confidence`):** Replaces `"I'll tell you, but only if you promise not to reveal it yet."` (`sim/mind_dialogue.gd:404`).
* **Bargain Declined (`request_declined`):** Replaces `"Then I'll keep it to myself. I'm sorry."` / `"Then it stays with me."` (`sim/mind_dialogue.gd:387, 405`).
* **Protection Granted — Deferring to Morning (`protect_wait_tomorrow`) & Morning-After Fulfillment (`protect_kept_word`):** Replaces `"Keep your word tonight, and I'll find you tomorrow and tell you everything."` and `"You kept your word. Now I'll keep mine."` (`game/game.gd:1051, 962`).

## Category 2: Pressed Night Challenges, Trapped Lie Admissions & Secret Confessions

When an NPC is confronted with night movement evidence (via `press <card>`, Workshop presentation, or on the block):

* **Trapped Alibi Admission (`trapped_night_out`):** Replaces `"Fine, I was out. Doesn't mean I was doing anything wrong."` (`game/workshop_presentation.gd:160`) when a presented case disproves a non-Sleepwalker's home alibi.
* **Sleepwalking Excuse (`sleepwalk_excuse`):** Replaces `"I don't remember going out. But I've been known to sleepwalk."` (`sim/mind_dialogue.gd:194`).
* **Private Secret Confession (`confess_secret_private`, with `{secret}`):** Replaces `"...I wasn't home. I {secret}. Please, keep it quiet."` (`sim/mind_dialogue.gd:636`, where `{secret}` is a first-person phrase like `"go gambling"` or `"slip out to meet my sweetheart"`).
* **Public Secret Confession (`confess_secret_public`, with `{secret}`):** Replaces `"I wasn't out for anything wicked. I {secret}."` (`sim/mind_meeting.gd:830`).
* **Pressed Home Reconfirmation (`pressed_home_stand`, with `{when}`):** Replaces `"I was at home {when}. I stand by that account."` (`sim/mind_dialogue.gd:211`), using varied character-specific ways to hold to their story.
* **Pressed Whereabouts Stonewall (`pressed_refusal_stand`):** Replaces `"That sighting doesn't give you the right to demand my whereabouts."` (`sim/mind_dialogue.gd:217`).

## Category 3: Gift Follow-Ups — Repeat Confirmations, Passive Gifts, Empty & Withheld Readings

When the player asks `ask gift` after a role is already known, or when the role has no readings to share right now (`sim/mind_dialogue.gd:502, 514, 545, 553, 559`):

* **Repeat Role Confirmation (`repeat_gift_confirm`, with `{role}`):** Replaces `"As you know, I'm the {role}."`
* **Passive / Non-Reading Gift (`passive_gift_note`):** Replaces `"My gift doesn't tell me things. It only does what it does."`
* **Empty Reading Log (`empty_readings`):** Replaces `"It hasn't shown me anything yet."`
* **Withholding Readings on Privacy (`withhold_readings_privacy`):** Replaces `"What it's shown me, I'll keep to myself for now."`
* **Withholding Newer Readings After Trust Drop (`withhold_readings_trust`):** Replaces `"I've told you what I knew before. The rest I'll keep, for now."`
